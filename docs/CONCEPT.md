# ATLANTIS — World concept (source of truth for the references and the build)

One city, two eras, built from the same layout:

* **Golden age** (`era=golden`): Plato's Atlantis at its height, late-afternoon golden light.
* **Sunken** (`era=sunken`): the same city ~ 9000 years later, 30–60 m under a clear tropical sea.

The video goes golden → surface crossing → sunken; matched compositions dissolve between eras.

## The city (after Plato, *Critias* 113–117, scaled for camera reading)

* **Central island / citadel**: a low rocky hill (≈ 400 m across, summit ≈ 45 m above the sea) ringed by
  an **orichalcum-clad wall** (red-gold metal, warm glow in sun). On the summit: the **Temple of Poseidon**.
* **Rings**: alternating rings around the island, outward: water ring 1 (≈ 60 m wide) → land ring 1
  (≈ 140 m, **tin-clad** wall: pale silver-grey) → water ring 2 (≈ 90 m) → land ring 2 (≈ 180 m,
  **bronze/brass-clad** outer wall) → water ring 3 (≈ 120 m) → the outer city on the main land.
* **Bridges**: arched stone bridges cross every water ring on the north–south axis; where a bridge meets a
  land ring a **tower and gate** stands on each side; the land rings are cut by covered canal **tunnels**
  so ships pass from ring to ring under the bridges.
* **Sea canal**: a straight canal (≈ 90 m wide) runs south from water ring 3 to the open sea, flanked by
  quays, lighthouse towers at the sea mouth, and a colossal **Poseidon with trident** on the mole.
* **Docks**: in the land rings, roofed ship-sheds cut into the rock face (arched openings at water level),
  full of **triremes** (dark hulls, eye painted on the bow, square cream/white sails, banks of oars).
* **Stone**: Plato's three stones — **white, black and red** — used in bands and patterns: walls with
  alternating white/red courses, black-stone bases, polychrome checker paving.
* **Temple of Poseidon**: big (≈ 90 × 45 m), on a stepped podium; outer walls clad in **silver**, pinnacles
  and acroteria in **gold**, roof ridge with gold tridents; ionic/aeolic columns with bronze capitals;
  in front, a colossal **Poseidon in a chariot drawn by winged horses** (bronze-gold), ringed by
  **Nereids riding dolphins**. Twin spring fountains (one steaming hot, one cold) feed basins and
  channels that run down the hill.
* **Other buildings**: palaces on the citadel, smaller temples, baths, gymnasia, a racecourse on land ring 2,
  houses with flat roofs and roof gardens, colonnaded streets, statues of the ten kings in gold.
* **Vegetation (golden)**: date palms, cypress, pines, olive, lush gardens and terraces, bougainvillea
  magenta, lotus/papyrus in basins.
* **Landscape**: the city sits on a coastal plain; behind it to the north a wall of tall green-grey
  mountains with cloud banks; to the south the open, deep-blue sea with islands.

## Sunken era (same layout, transformed)

* Sea level is ≈ 55 m above the golden-age level: the temple roof is ≈ 10 m below the surface, the outer
  city ≈ 50–60 m down on white sand. Visibility 60–90 m; blue-green water, colour lost with depth.
* Light: sun shafts (god rays) from the rippled surface, moving **caustics** on every upward face,
  suspended particles (marine snow), deep blue fog with distance.
* Damage: roofs partly collapsed, columns toppled in rows, statues fallen (a giant fallen bronze
  Poseidon head with the broken trident beside it), walls cracked; sand drifts against walls.
* Growth: corals (brain, plate, branching), sea fans, sponges, anemones, kelp forests on the outer
  rings, sea grass on sand; orichalcum and bronze now **verdigris green-blue**, gold still glinting.
* Life: schools of silver fish, a few rays, turtles; no people.

## Palette

* Golden: white stone cream-gold in sun, cool violet-blue shadows; orichalcum red-gold; water turquoise
  in the rings, deep cobalt sea; vegetation deep green; sky warm gold → blue.
* Sunken: teal → deep blue (#0b3d5c → #04182b with depth), sand pale cyan-white, coral muted pink/orange
  close to camera only, verdigris; highlights from caustics and rays.
