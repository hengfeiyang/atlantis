Grand aerial view of the legendary city of Atlantis at its golden age, seen from high above the open sea to the south, late afternoon golden sun from the left. The city is perfectly concentric: a central rocky island-hill with a large silver-clad Temple of Poseidon with gold pinnacles on its summit, ringed by an orichalcum (red-gold metal) clad wall; around it alternating rings of turquoise water and green land: water ring, land ring with a pale tin-clad wall, wider water ring, wider land ring with a bronze-clad wall, outer water ring, then the outer city. Arched stone bridges with gate towers cross every water ring on one axis. A straight wide sea canal runs from the outer ring toward the camera to the sea, flanked by quays and lighthouse towers, with triremes with square white sails. Buildings use bands of white, black and red stone; date palms and cypress gardens; a racecourse on the outer land ring. Behind the city a coastal plain and a wall of tall green-grey mountains with cloud banks. Deep cobalt sea in the foreground. Style: photoreal cinematic matte painting / high-end film concept art, natural colours, not oversaturated, rich detail everywhere, no text, no people, no watermark, wide 16:9.
