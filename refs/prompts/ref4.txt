Atlantis, golden age: view along the inner water ring of the concentric city, from a boat at water level. On the right, the land ring rises as a sheer rock face with a row of arched rock-cut ship-sheds at water level, each holding a trireme bow-out; above them a tall wall clad in pale silver-grey tin with towers. Ahead an arched stone bridge crosses the ring, and a dark covered canal tunnel cuts through the land ring. On the left above the water, the citadel hill with an orichalcum red-gold wall, palaces and the silver Temple of Poseidon at the top. Calm turquoise water reflecting the walls. Late afternoon golden light, warm and cool contrast. Style: photoreal cinematic matte painting / high-end film concept art, natural colours, not oversaturated, rich detail everywhere, no text, no people, no watermark, wide 16:9.
